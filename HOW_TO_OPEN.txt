BrainSenser - how to open it
============================

BrainSenser isn't code-signed yet, so macOS and Windows warn you the first
time you open it. That's expected; here is how to get past it.

MACOS (14 Sonoma or later)
  Which file: Apple menu > About This Mac.
    "Chip: Apple M..."     -> BrainSenser-<version>-silicon.dmg
    "Processor: Intel..."  -> BrainSenser-<version>-intel.dmg

  1. Double-click the .dmg and drag BrainSenser onto Applications.
  2. Open BrainSenser from Applications. macOS blocks it the first time.
  3. Go to System Settings > Privacy & Security, scroll down, and click
     "Open Anyway" next to the BrainSenser message. Confirm.
  Or, instead of steps 2-3, run this once in Terminal:
     xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine /Applications/BrainSenser.app

WINDOWS (10 or 11, 64-bit)
  1. Right-click the zip > Extract All. Keep the whole BrainSenser folder
     together; the app needs everything in it. A short path such as
     Documents\BrainSenser works best.
  2. Open BrainSenser\BrainSenser.exe.
  3. If "Windows protected your PC" appears, click "More info", then
     "Run anyway".
  If Windows says the app was blocked by Smart App Control, it can't run on
  that PC until BrainSenser is code-signed.

FIRST LAUNCH
  The first start can take up to a minute while the plotting library builds
  its font cache. Later starts are faster.

YOUR DATA
  BrainSenser ships with no patient data. What you import stays on your
  computer:
    macOS:   ~/Library/Application Support/BrainSenser
    Windows: %LOCALAPPDATA%\BrainSenser
  Updating the app never deletes it. To remove it, delete that folder.
